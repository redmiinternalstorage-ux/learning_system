9. UNDER MAINTENANCE SYSTEM SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Middleware: `App\Http\Middleware\CheckMaintenanceMode`
- Logic:
  * Checks `SiteSetting::get('maintenance_mode')`.
  * If active (1), non-admin public visitors & students are intercepted and served `resources/views/maintenance.blade.php` (HTTP Status 503).
  * Admins, Staff, and Finance roles bypass the middleware and can access the platform & login routes (`/login`, `/admin*`).
- Maintenance View (`resources/views/maintenance.blade.php`):
  * Deep Forest Dark Theme (`#04140e`).
  * Animated spinning maintenance gears icon.

  * Description: Explaining scheduled system upgrades.
